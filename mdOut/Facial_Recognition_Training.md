Image Collection Guidelines for Facial Recognition Training

Overview

This document outlines the requirements and best practices for collectin=g training images for facial recognition systems. Following these guideline=s will help ensure optimal model performance and accuracy.

Minimum Requirements

** _Quantity_**

- Minimum Images Per Person: 10 high-quality images

- Recommended Images Per Person: 15-20 images for better model robustness<=/p>

- Face Coverage: Face should occupy 60-70% of frame

- Minimum Resolution: 224x224 pixels \(higher is better\)

- Image Quality Specifications

- File Format: JPG or PNG \(uncompressed preferred\)

- Image Size: Minimum 1MB per image for adequate detail

** _Face Positioning_**

- Primary angles: Front-facing and 3/4 profile views

- Consistent eye line \(looking at camera\)

- Natural head position \(no extreme tilts\)

- Clear view of both eyes when possible

** _Lighting Conditions_**

- Even, diffused lighting

- No harsh shadows across face

- Avoid backlit situations

- Minimum 100 lux \(bright enough to read comfortably\)

**_Image Clarity_**

- Sharp focus on facial features

- No motion blur

- No excessive digital noise

- Clean lens and sensor

** _Variety Requirements_**

- Lighting Variations

- Indoor artificial lighting

- Natural daylight

- Overcast conditions

- Side lighting

- Front lighting

** _Expression Variations_**

- Neutral expression \(40% of images\)

- Natural smile \(30% of images\)

- Other expressions \(30% of images\):

- Serious

- Laughing

- Speaking

- Professional demeanor

** _Angle Variations_**

- Direct front face \(40% of images\)

- 3/4 left profile \(20% of images\)

- 3/4 right profile \(20% of images\)

- Slight upward angle \(10% of images\)

- Slight downward angle \(10% of images\)

- Context Variations

** _Different backgrounds_**

- Various clothing/accessories

- Different times of day

- Multiple locations

** _What to Avoid_**

- Blurry or out-of-focus images

- Over or underexposed photos

- Heavy digital compression artifacts

- Resolution below 224x224 pixels

- Excessive noise or grain

- Composition Issues \(nothing covering any part of the face\)

- Face occupying less than 50% of frame

- Extreme angles \(>45 degrees from front\)

- Heavy shadows across face

- Excessive glare or reflections

- Multiple faces in close proximity

- Sunglasses or protective eyewear

- Face masks or coverings

- Objects blocking facial features

- Heavy makeup that alters face shape

- Extreme facial expressions that distort features

** _Best Practices for Image Collection_**

- Use a camera with minimum 12MP resolution

- Clean lens before shooting

- Use tripod or stabilization when possible

- Avoid heavy post-processing or filters

** _Before submitting images, verify:_**

\[ \] Minimum quantity met per person

\[ \] Resolution requirements met

\[ \] Proper variety across all categories

\[ \] No technical issues present

\[ \] Files properly named and organized

\[ \] Backups created and stored

\[ \] Model-specific requirements met

- ** _Environment Setup_**

- Choose well-lit locations

- Use neutral backgrounds when possible

- Maintain consistent distance from subject

- Ensure adequate space for proper framing

Collection Process

Plan shoots across multiple days

Vary time of day for natural lighting differences

Mix indoor and outdoor locations

Document shooting conditions

Review images immediately for quality

Maintain backup copies of all images
